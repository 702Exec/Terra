## Notes

Cinema mode M5 (Atmospheric). This is the revised launch that produced the approved
render — legs locked closed, spear silhouette, single simultaneous clamp release.

**The camera is deliberately locked off.** No handheld, no push, no drift. All motion in
frame comes from the machine. This is what makes the cut to the handheld ground shots
land — the contrast between a camera that doesn't move and one that can barely hold on.
Don't add camera movement here without re-thinking that transition.

**Phrases doing the legs-closed work.** If the legs ever start deploying in a render,
these are the lines holding the line — strengthen rather than replace them:

- "folded tight and locked completely flush against the hull"
- "one smooth unbroken spear shape with no limbs protruding anywhere along its length"
- "never open, never unfold, never deploy"
- "the silhouette staying a single clean tapered spear the whole way down"

**Thrown, not released.** "A single hard simultaneous snap" and "thrown straight down…
like a spear cast at the planet, accelerating fast and cleanly along its own axis with no
tumble and no yaw" are what separate this from the machine simply drifting out of a bay.
If a render feels passive, that clause is the one to push on.

**Sequence position.** Shot 1 of 3 in the 20s cut (5 + 10 + 5). Legs stay closed through
shot 2 and deploy only during the shot 3 descent.
