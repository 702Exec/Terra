# The Ancients: Unit Reference & Technical Specifications

This reference document details the full combat roster for **The Ancients**. It details their tactical roles, biological/mechanical classification, unique abilities, and their functional *StarCraft II* mechanical equivalents for development parity.

---

## 🏛️ Gateway Units (The Martial Order)
Produced or manifested via the **Aether Conduit** / **Manifestation Conduit**.

### 1. Paladin
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Zealot
*   **Classification:** Biological (Psionic-Enhanced Armor)
*   **Role:** Frontline Melee Vanguard
*   **Description:** The physical elite of The Ancients. Armed with twin focalized starlight blades, they slice through armor with absolute precision. Their heavy energy shields allow them to absorb tremendous frontline pressure, absorbing incoming kinetic energy to fuel their movements.
*   **Core Ability:** *Kinetic Rush* – Passively increases movement speed. Active: Automatically charges forward at high speed to lock onto a targeted enemy when within striking distance.

### 2. Rift-Stalker
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Stalker
*   **Classification:** Mechanical (Cybernetic Shell / Preserved Consciousness)
*   **Role:** Ranged Anti-Air & Anti-Armor Skirmisher
*   **Description:** Striking quadrupedal constructs piloted by the preserved minds of fallen Ancient heroes. Firing high-impact streams of phase particles, they excel at kiting heavier targets and tracking fast-moving airborne threats.
*   **Core Ability:** *Quantum Shifting* – Instantly dematerializes and teleports a short distance across terrain or into tactical positions. (Cooldown: 10 seconds).

### 3. Aegis Vanguard
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Sentry
*   **Classification:** Robotic Support Unit
*   **Role:** Tactical Field Manipulator & Shield Support
*   **Description:** A levitating robotic drone designed to Anchor and project the localized Aether Matrix onto the battlefield. While lacking significant offensive weapons, its energy projection systems alter the physics of the combat zone to protect organic ground troops.
*   **Core Abilities:**
    *   *Force Barrier* – Projects an impenetrable hard-light wall at a target location for 15 seconds to block choke points or split enemy forces.
    *   *Aether Shield* – Emits a bubble dome that reduces all incoming ranged damage to friendly units inside by 20%.

### 4. Justiciar
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Adept
*   **Classification:** Biological (Ranged Skirmisher)
*   **Role:** Light Infantry Harasser & Scout
*   **Description:** Elite marksmen who wield pneumatic starlight carbines. They excel at penetrating light armor and breaking up infantry formations, relying on projection technology to escape dangerous situations rather than raw shield strength.
*   **Core Ability:** *Spectral Mirage* – Projects an un-targetable invulnerable shade clone forward for 7 seconds. At the end of the duration, the Justiciar instantly teleports to the shade's exact location. The player can cancel the projection early.

## ⚙️ Robotics Facility (The Cyber-Constructs)
Manufactured physically on-site via the **Automaton Foundry**.

### 5. Glimmer Drone
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Probe
*   **Classification:** Robotic Worker
*   **Role:** Resource Harvester & Manifestation Anchor
*   **Description:** The fundamental automated workforce of The Ancients. Hovering slightly off the ground, they gather planetary raw elements and open localized micro-rifts to warp in structural anchors, freeing them to return to work immediately.
*   **Core Ability:** *Rift Anchor* – Summons a structure instantly by opening an Aether portal, requiring no channel or build time from the drone itself.

### 6. Seer-Drone
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Observer
*   **Classification:** Robotic Reconnaissance
*   **Role:** Cloaked Detector & Scout
*   **Description:** A miniature, lightweight flying sensor drone equipped with light-bending active camouflage. It drifts silently over enemy territory, feeding structural weakness details and structural mapping telemetry back to the network.
*   **Core Abilities:**
    *   *Permanent Cloaking* – Passively invisible to the naked eye; requires specialized enemy detection to reveal.
    *   *True Sight* – Reveals all cloaked, burrowed, or hidden enemy structures and units within its vision radius.

### 7. Sentinel
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Immortal
*   **Classification:** Mechanical Assault Walker
*   **Role:** Heavy Anti-Armor Frontline
*   **Description:** Massive twin-cannon combat walkers reserved for breaking fortified armored lines. They are deployed when organic shock troopers meet heavily mechanized defenses, safely absorbing devastating blasts that would vaporize typical infantry.
*   **Core Ability:** *Hard-Light Barrier* – Passively triggers an emergency energy barrier when taking damage, absorbing up to a large threshold of incoming damage for 10 seconds before cracking. (Cooldown: 30 seconds).

### 8. Warp Prism
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Warp Prism
*   **Classification:** Robotic Transport & Logistic Hub
*   **Role:** Aerial Troop Transport & Mobile Power Grid
*   **Description:** A versatile flying crystal lattice used to split and redeploy strike teams across vast planetary spaces. It serves as a vital tactical anchor for deep operations far away from established base infrastructure.
*   **Core Ability:** *Matrix Anchor* – Transforms into a stationary pylon mode, anchoring and projecting an active Aether Matrix power field directly underneath it to allow remote *Aetherial Manifestation* (Warp-In) of units.

### 9. Apex Strider
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Colossus
*   **Classification:** Heavy Robotic Siege Platform
*   **Role:** Area-of-Effect Anti-Infantry Siege Mech
*   **Description:** A towering, multi-legged mechanical colossus built to step over cliffs and clear vast swarms of ground troops. Its dual thermal lances sweep horizontally across the battlefield, melting organic infantry lines in seconds.
*   **Core Abilities:**
    *   *Cliff Scaling* – Passively steps up and down cliffs or elevation changes seamlessly without requiring ramps.
    *   *Thermal Sweep* – Fires twin beams that trace a horizontal line, dealing massive area-of-effect damage to all ground units caught in the path.

## 🌌 Astral Fleet (The Starships)
Summoned from orbit via the **Astral Shipyard**.

### 10. Interseptor
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Phoenix
*   **Classification:** Mechanical Starfighter
*   **Role:** High-Speed Anti-Air Air Dominance Fighter
*   **Description:** A sleek, rapid-response atmospheric starfighter that fires twin ion pulses. It cannot attack ground targets directly under normal circumstances, but uses gravity manipulation to pull ground units into its firing window.
*   **Core Ability:** *Graviton Beam* – Lifts a target enemy ground unit into the air for 10 seconds, rendering it completely helpless and allowing other friendly air units to attack it. Disables the Interseptor's weapons while channeling.

### 11. Eclipse Ship
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Void Ray
*   **Classification:** Mechanical Capital Ship
*   **Role:** Focused Anti-Armor Air Destroyer
*   **Description:** Designed around a massive, floating central focusing crystal. The ship channels a continuous stream of prismatic energy that destabilizes heavy molecular bonds, compounding its damage the longer it stays locked onto a single target.
*   **Core Ability:** *Prismatic Overload* – Increases damage against heavily armored units and structures by 50% for 20 seconds, but slightly reduces flight movement speed while active.

### 12. Nebula
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Oracle
*   **Classification:** Mechanical Tactical Support
*   **Role:** Raiding, Scout, & Crowd-Control Starship
*   **Description:** A fast-moving, high-tech tactical ship utilizing cosmic energy manipulation to disrupt enemy operational layouts and trap defensive groups.
*   **Core Abilities:**
    *   *Cosmic Ray* – Consumes energy to fire a high-intensity energy beam capable of rapidly liquefying light ground infantry.
    *   *Stasis Trap* – Plants an invisible underground device that freezes all enemy units within its radius in stasis for 15 seconds when stepped on.
    *   *Aetheric Vision* – Grants vision of targeted enemy units or structures through fog of war for 60 seconds.

### 13. Supernova
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Tempest
*   **Classification:** Heavy Capital Ship
*   **Role:** Ultra-Long-Range Capital Siege Ship
*   **Description:** A massive, slow-moving orbital platform built exclusively to dismantle planetary static defenses and enemy flagships from supreme, untouchable distances.
*   **Core Ability:** *Kinetic Slingshot* – Passively allows its main structural battery to fire from extreme ranges, far out-ranging all planetary static base defense structures.

### 14. Arkship
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Carrier
*   **Classification:** Heavy Capital Flagship
*   **Role:** Fleet Flagship & Automated Swarm Carrier
*   **Description:** The ultimate symbol of The Ancients' technological dominance. The Arkship acts as a mobile factory platform, housing automated foundries that build and launch swarms of miniature automated interceptors to overwhelm defenses.
*   **Core Ability:** *Launch Drone-Fighters* – Automatically deploys up to 8 small, autonomous fighter drones to swarm, attack, and distract enemy anti-air grids. Drones automatically rebuild inside the launch bay when destroyed.

## 🌀 High-Tech Citadel (The Ascended Masters)
Manifested via high-tier psionic structures (**Grand Sanctuary** / **Shrouded Obelisk**).

### 15. Acolyte
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** High Templar
*   **Classification:** Biological (Psionic Master)
*   **Role:** Area-of-Effect Psionic Caster
*   **Description:** Monks who have completely abandoned physical weapons to master the raw, fundamental power of the cosmic mind. They are physically frail but can completely shatter armies using elemental forces.
*   **Core Abilities:**
    *   *Astral Storm* – Conjures a violent storm of psionic lightning over a designated area for 3 seconds, dealing heavy damage to all enemy units caught inside.
    *   *Overload* – Drains all energy from a target enemy caster unit, dealing damage to it equal to the amount of energy removed.

### 16. Spectre
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Dark Templar
*   **Classification:** Biological (Stealth Assassin)
*   **Role:** Infiltrator & High-Value Target Executioner
*   **Description:** Deadly warriors who utilize the shadows of the void to remain hidden. Wielding massive, dual-bladed light scythes, they specialize in bypassing frontline forces to decapitate enemy commanders or wipe out economic resource extraction nodes.
*   **Core Abilities:**
    *   *Permanent Cloaking* – Passively invisible to the naked eye at all times; requires specialized enemy detection to reveal.
    *   *Shadow Slash* – An upgradeable strike that allows the Spectre to blink instantly through a row of infantry, slicing all targets in a straight path.

### 17. Avatar
*   **SC2 Equivalent:** Archon
*   **Classification:** Psionic Energy Entity
*   **Role:** Heavy Powerhouse / Splash-Damage Shock Trooper
*   **Description:** Born of ultimate sacrifice when two Acolytes, two Spectres, or a combination of both permanently merge their souls. The result is a terrifying, glowing entity of pure starlight energy held together by sheer willpower, boasting near-impenetrable energy shielding.
*   **Core Abilities:**
    *   *Psionic Shockwave* – Attacks deal heavy area-of-effect splash damage to all air and ground targets immediately surrounding the primary target.
    *   *Massive Shields* – Features massive plasma shield capacity, but possesses virtually no physical health points once the shield layer collapses.
