# The Ancients: 20-Level Campaign Progression Design Document

This design document outlines the 20-level campaign for **The Ancients**, an advanced, psionic alien race desperately searching for resources to save their dying home planet. Each level takes place on a **real, scientifically recognized planet or moon**, matching its actual composition to real-world elements. 

The campaign builds up systematically, unlocking one unit or structure per level. It culminates in **Level 20 on Earth**, the ultimate "boss planet" containing all vital elements but defended by unified, global human militaries.

## 🪐 Detailed Level Breakdowns

### Level 1: Mars
*   **Target Location:** The rust-red deserts of the Red Planet.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Iron** (Extracted from the vast iron-oxide surface dust).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Sovereign Spire** *(Nexus)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Establish the initial beachhead. Build your command hub and protect it from planetary dust storms.

### Level 2: Mercury
*   **Target Location:** The heavily cratered, sun-scorched crust closest to the Sun.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Zinc** (Mined from core-exposed impact basins).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Matrix Pylon** *(Pylon)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Power management mission. Learn to link pylons together across thermal lines to power structures before freezing during the Mercurian night.

### Level 3: Enceladus (Moon of Saturn)
*   **Target Location:** The sub-surface ocean vents of Saturn's icy moon.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Water** (Siphoned from spectacular active cryovolcanic plumes).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Glimmer Drone** *(Probe)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Economic ramp-up. Optimize worker production lines to harvest erupting water geysers before they freeze solid over the collection zones.

### Level 4: Ceres
*   **Target Location:** The largest asteroid and dwarf planet in the asteroid belt.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Water & Iron** (Extracted from the dark clay crust and water-ice mantle).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Aether Conduit** *(Gateway)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Set up your foundational troop manufacturing arrays in low gravity while fending off rogue asteroid-mining automated defensive bots.

### Level 5: Venus
*   **Target Location:** The crushing atmospheric pressure and sulfuric acid clouds of Venus.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Sulfur** (Harvested directly from active volcanic calderas).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Paladin** *(Zealot)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Survival deployment. Your newly unlocked Paladins must hold chokepoints against local mechanical scavengers while the acidic atmosphere slowly degrades unpowered outer hulls.

### Level 6: Psyche
*   **Target Location:** The unique, exposed metallic core asteroid orbiting between Mars and Jupiter.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Nickel & Silver** (Stripped from massive, exposed iron-nickel metal fields).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Solari Forge** *(Forge)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Technological breakthrough. Establish the Forge to weaponize the raw planetary metals, unlocking core weapon and shield upgrades for your Paladins.

### Level 7: Titan (Moon of Saturn)
*   **Target Location:** The dense, hazy nitrogen-rich atmosphere and liquid hydrocarbon seas.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Methane & Nitrogen** (Pumped directly from freezing liquid lakes like Kraken Mare).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Monolith Turret** *(Photon Cannon)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Defensive holdout. Protect massive automated fuel siphons from relentless waves of ancient automated drones by creating tight chokepoints with Monolith Turrets.

### Level 8: Ganymede (Moon of Jupiter)
*   **Target Location:** The largest moon in the Solar System, possessing its own magnetic field.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Water & Silicates** (Slipped from the thick layer of ice separating the rocky mantle).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Cybernetics Core** *(Cybernetics Core)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Infiltrate underground ice labyrinths to recover ancient data caches, unlocking advanced processing power via the Cybernetics Core.

### Level 9: Io (Moon of Jupiter)
*   **Target Location:** The most geologically active body in the Solar System, torn by Jupiter's gravity.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Sulfur & Copper** (Harvested from volatile, yellow-orange lava lakes).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Rift-Stalker** *(Stalker)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Extreme mobility test. Utilize your newly unlocked Rift-Stalkers to bypass magma chasms using their short-range teleportation ("Quantum Shifting") while harvesting volatile elements.

### Level 10: Europa (Moon of Jupiter)
*   **Target Location:** The smooth, cracked ice sheet concealing a massive subsurface ocean.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Water** (Drilled from deep hydrothermal ocean shafts).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Aegis Vanguard** *(Sentry)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Tactical crowd control. Defend deep-core thermal drills using Aegis Vanguards to throw up protective barriers against waves of fast-moving security bots tracking your heat signatures.

### Level 11: Callisto (Moon of Jupiter)
*   **Target Location:** The heavily cratered, ancient ball of ice and rock.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Carbon & Iron** (Mined from deeply buried primitive impact craters).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Justiciar** *(Adept)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Stealth and scouting. Use the Justiciar's holographic mirage ability to bypass high-frequency radar nets and ping rich subterranean carbon vaults.

### Level 12: Triton (Moon of Neptune)
*   **Target Location:** The frozen moon with a retrograde orbit, active geysers, and a cantaloupe-skin surface.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Nitrogen Ice** (Gathered from erupting nitrogen gas spouts).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Automaton Foundry** *(Robotics Facility)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Heavy machinery setup. Construct the Automaton Foundry to begin moving beyond pure biological/energy troops into massive cyber-constructs.

### Level 13: Pluto
*   **Target Location:** The distant Kuiper Belt dwarf planet of nitrogen ice mountains.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Methane Ice** (Scraped from frozen glaciers across the dark Cthulhu Macula region).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Seer-Drone** *(Observer)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Deep reconnaissance. Navigate through total darkness and sensor-scrambling cosmic storms using invisible, cloaked Seer-Drones to safely locate the hidden ice caches.

### Level 14: Proxima Centauri b
*   **Target Location:** The closest known exoplanet, tidally locked to its red dwarf star.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Sodium** (Mined from salt-crusted desert flats in the permanent twilight zone).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Sentinel** *(Immortal)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Direct assault. Use the massive energy shields of your new Sentinel heavy walkers to absorb high-impact heavy artillery fire from entrenched planetary outposts.

### Level 15: Kepler-186f
*   **Target Location:** The historic first validated Earth-sized planet in a distant star's habitable zone.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Carbon & Silicon** (Mined from rich, red-tinted mountain forests).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Archon Vault** *(Robotics Bay)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Tech development. Defend your research facilities from localized defense systems long enough to build the Archon Vault and upgrade heavy vehicular weaponry.

### Level 16: Gliese 581c
*   **Target Location:** A super-Earth exoplanet experiences extreme tidal locking and severe gravity.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Iron & Water** (Extracted from deep boiling oceans facing the red dwarf sun).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Apex Strider** *(Colossus)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** High-ground dominance. Use the towering, multi-legged Apex Striders to step over treacherous, jagged volcanic cliffs and sweep thermal lances down onto swarming ground forces.

### Level 17: 55 Cancri e
*   **Target Location:** A carbon-rich super-Earth so intensely hot that its surface is a sea of molten lava.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Diamond / Carbon** (Mined from thick underground diamond structural layers).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Twilight Crucible** *(Twilight Council)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** High-tier ascension. Advance your infantry forces by unlocking the Twilight Crucible, allowing troops to move faster and channel cosmic elements more effectively.

### Level 18: TRAPPIST-1e
*   **Target Location:** An ultra-compact rocky world sitting perfectly in the sweet spot of an active seven-planet system.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Water & Magnesium** (Extracted from pristine, Earth-like liquid oceans).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Grand Sanctuary** *(Templar Archives)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Protect the sacred convergence point. Build a Grand Sanctuary to unlock the deepest secrets of psionic storm manipulation while being attacked from multiple planetary directions.

### Level 19: Kepler-22b
*   **Target Location:** A massive water-world planet completely covered by a global, deep ocean.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **Water & Ocean Minerals** (Siphoned from heavy deep-sea geothermal vents).
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Acolyte** *(High Templar)*.
*   **Mission Profile:** Ascended dominance. Bring forth the Acolytes. Use their devastating *Astral Storm* abilities to evaporate incoming swarms of flying sea-skimmers and automated security platforms.

### Level 20: Earth (The Final Boss Planet)
*   **Target Location:** A vibrant, blue-green world pulsing with life, liquid water, and fully developed technological civilization.
*   **Harvested Resource:** **ALL ELEMENTS COMBINED** (Water, Gold, Silver, Platinum, Uranium, Iron). 
*   **Unlocked Asset:** **Full Astral Fleet Unlocked** (Arkships, Nebulas, Supernovas brought down to face the threat).
*   **Mission Profile:** **The Ultimate Harvest.** Earth possesses the precise cocktail of heavy minerals and pure liquid water required to fully reignite the dying home world of The Ancients. However, humanity is fully aware of your arrival. You face a unified, global coalition defense force equipped with kinetic nuclear options, orbital railguns, and overwhelming tactical aircraft squads. You must establish a massive planetary extraction matrix and defend it to complete the final jump back home.
